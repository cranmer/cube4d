MagicCube4D

A four-dimensional Rubik’s cube, in your browser.

A modern port of the original, by Kyle Cranmer and Claude. Every one of the 128 puzzles it can build is here, and solve files from the original open and replay unchanged.

Classic

The full catalog — all 128 puzzles, free rotation, save and load, and the Hall of Fame records replaying at your own pace. Closest to the original.

Hypercube

A layout built around the 3×3×3×3 itself rather than around a catalog, with named viewpoints and more than one view at once.

Source and porting log on GitHub

The original MagicCube4D is by Melinda Green, Don Hatch, Jay Berkenbilt and Roice Nelson.
